Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Google Sheets Automation with n8n ("the Application") handles information when users authorize Google account access for workflow automation.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the Application may access Google account information and Google Sheets data required for the configured workflows. The exact information accessible depends on the Google OAuth scopes authorized by the user.

2. How We Use Google Data

Google user data is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user, including connecting Google Sheets to n8n and executing authorized automation workflows. We do not use Google user data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. n8n and Data Processing

Authorized Google data may be processed by n8n when a configured workflow runs. Depending on the workflow and hosting configuration, data may pass through workflow executions, logs, connected services, or temporary storage. Users should configure workflows and retention settings appropriately and avoid sending unnecessary personal information to other services.

4. Data Sharing

Data is shared with services only when necessary to perform the workflows configured by the user or to operate and secure the Application. We do not sell Google user data. Any third-party service connected to a workflow may process data according to its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Data Storage and Retention

OAuth credentials and workflow data may be stored or retained by the configured n8n environment, depending on its settings. Retention periods depend on workflow execution history, credential storage, backups, and other configured services. Data should be deleted when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect OAuth credentials and workflow data. Access to credentials should be restricted to authorized users. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke the Application's Google account access through their Google Account third-party connections settings. Revoking access prevents future API requests that require the revoked authorization. It does not automatically delete previously stored data, workflow execution records, or backups.

Google account access can be reviewed at: Google Account Connections .

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended to be directed at children. Do not provide children's personal information unless there is an appropriate legal basis and the necessary safeguards are in place.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application's functionality, data practices, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact
