Google Sheets Automation with n8n

This application enables users to connect Google Sheets with n8n to automate spreadsheet-related tasks and integrate Google Sheets into their workflows.

What Does This Application Do?

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authorize access to Google services selected by the user. Authorized access allows configured n8n workflows to interact with Google Sheets according to the permissions granted.

How It Works

Users authorize Google access through Google's consent screen. The resulting credentials are configured in n8n and used to run the workflows that the user sets up. Access is limited by the granted permissions and the workflow configuration.

Privacy and Security

Google account access is authorized by the user. Google data is handled according to the application's intended functionality and the configured workflows. Please review our Privacy Policy for details.
